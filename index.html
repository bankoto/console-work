How the Console Works

One dashboard. AI drafts it, a professional signs it.

Every task in your Console follows the same loop — nothing reaches you or the government without a licensed professional checking it first.

01

Request lands in your task feed

Submit a request — or let a routine task (a filing deadline, a payroll cycle) trigger automatically. It shows up in your Console instantly.

→
02

AI drafts it in real time

Bankoto's AI agents draft the filing, reconcile the numbers, or prepare the document — you can watch progress live in the Console.

→
03

Your expert reviews and signs off

A licensed accountant, HR professional or lawyer assigned to your account checks the work against standard before anything is filed, paid or sent.

Why the Console

Technology for speed. Professionals for judgment.

The Console isn't a chatbot and it isn't a form. It's where AI's speed and a licensed professional's judgment meet on every single task.
